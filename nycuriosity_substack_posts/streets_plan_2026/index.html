Local Law 195 — Streets Plan Data

Fiscal impact estimates, compliance tracking, and program-level cost breakdowns for Local Law 195 of 2019 (Intro 1557-A), NYC's mandate for a 10-year master plan for streets, bike lanes, bus lanes, and pedestrian infrastructure. Data sourced from the NYC Council Finance Division fiscal impact statement and DOT Streets Plan progress reports. Compiled for NYCuriosity's Streets Plan analysis.
