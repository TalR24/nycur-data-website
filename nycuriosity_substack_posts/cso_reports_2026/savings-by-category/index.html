Savings by Category

How the $246M in total CSO savings break down by type of action, combining FY26 and FY27 figures across all 16 agencies. Nearly half comes from a single category: eligibility audits that remove ineligible recipients from city programs.

Download CSV (members)

Categorization methodology: Each agency is assigned to its primary savings lever. DSS ($12.7M) is categorized under contract termination due to the McKinsey contract cancellation, though its plan also includes IT in-sourcing and WiFi revenue. H+H ($39.8M) is categorized under contract renegotiation (the dominant mechanism); its plan also includes staffing and revenue improvements. FDNY ($13M) is categorized under revenue & billing as Medicaid billing is the largest FY27 driver.

Sources: Agency CSO reports released March 20, 2026. FY26 and FY27 savings figures from NYC CSO agency submissions. Created by Tal Roded · NYCuriosity
