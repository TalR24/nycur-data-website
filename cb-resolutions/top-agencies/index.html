Top Agencies Mentioned in Resolutions

Which City and State agencies do the selected community boards address most? Counts how many resolutions mention each agency. Stacked segments show each board's share of the combined total.

Top agencies mentioned in NYC Community Board resolutions. A resolution can mention multiple agencies. Source: MCB2 + MCB3 meeting PDFs. Created by Tal Roded · NYCuriosity

Methodology

Each resolution's full text was scanned for the agency name and common abbreviations (e.g. "DOT" or "Department of Transportation"). Each resolution-agency pair counts once regardless of how many times the agency appears in the text.

Because a single resolution may mention multiple agencies, the column counts do not sum to the total resolution count.

Data

11,000+ resolutions across MCB2 and MCB3. Sources: MCB2 hub, MCB3 minutes.
