Civic Reference Updated monthly from NYC Legistar

Council legislation, tracked from price tag to to-do list

Two interactive trackers built from NYC Legistar, refreshed monthly. The Fiscal Impacts Tracker reads every bill's fiscal impact statement to show what legislation costs. The Legislation Implementation Tracker reads every enacted local law to show what it requires city agencies to actually do. The Council Members page connects both to the people who write the bills.
