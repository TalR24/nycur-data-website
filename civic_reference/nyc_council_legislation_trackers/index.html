Three trackers on what Council legislation costs, requires, and allows

Council legislation since 2014, read three ways. The Fiscal Impacts Tracker follows what each bill costs the city, from the Finance Division's own estimates. The Obligations Tracker lists the duties each local law places on city agencies, with deadlines. The Powers Tracker lists the authority each law hands those agencies. Each tracker has its own charts, tables, and member alerts.
