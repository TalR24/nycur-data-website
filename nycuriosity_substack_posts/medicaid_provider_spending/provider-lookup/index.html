This is the "how much did Company X make" table. Type any provider's name and get its Medicaid payments by year. Rows expand on click to show all seven years. Payments are facts, not accusations; use the methodology to understand exactly what they cover.

Coverage: billing organizations with NPPES practice locations in NY, TX, or CA and at least $100,000 in total 2018 to 2024 paid amounts. The floor keeps the page fast; it drops 87,645 small billers holding 0.5% of dollars. The downloadable CSV has the same columns for all providers above the floor.

Reading the numbers: figures are Medicaid and CHIP paid amounts on outpatient and professional claim lines with HCPCS codes, from the federal T-MSIS system. They exclude institutional per-diem and most pharmacy claims. 2024 is preliminary. A provider's absence means only that its billing NPI fell below the floor or sits in another state.

Sources: HHS Open Data, Medicaid Provider Spending by HCPCS; CMS NPPES (July 2026). Author's analysis; replication steps in the methodology.
