* OLR anomaly: The Office of Labor Relations' budget ($62.6M) reflects only its administrative operations. The $100M in FY27 savings from removing ineligible dependents comes from the citywide employee health benefits fund, which OLR administers on behalf of all city agencies — a pool vastly larger than OLR's own budget. The 159.9% figure is therefore not meaningful as a benchmark.

† MONS budget: The Mayor's Office of Nonprofit Services does not appear as a standalone appropriation in the NYC FY2025 Adopted Budget. It operates within the broader Mayoralty budget envelope; no isolated figure is available from published OMB data.

‡ % of FY25 budget basis: For agencies with FY27 savings, the percentage uses FY27 savings (the ongoing annual target). For agencies with FY26 savings only, it uses FY26 savings.

Sources: Savings figures from agency CSO reports released March 20, 2026. Budget figures from NYC OpenData Expense Budget (mwzb-yiwb), NYC OMB FY2025 Adopted Budget.
