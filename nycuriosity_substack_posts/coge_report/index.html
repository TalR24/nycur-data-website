Government Efficiency August 2026

What Mamdani's Efficiency Commission Put on the Ballot

Mayor Mamdani convened the Commission on Government Efficiency in May 2026. It studied twelve areas of the City Charter, adopted its final report unanimously on July 23, and sent five questions to voters this November. These charts cover what is on the ballot, and the procurement and technology problems the Charter could not reach.
