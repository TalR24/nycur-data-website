Overtime hit a record $2.85 billion and ran $299 million over budget

Overtime grew for the fifth straight year in fiscal 2026. The uniformed agencies spent $2.32 billion. Civilian agencies spent $532 million and produced two thirds of the overrun.

The State Comptroller estimates the City's out-year overtime budgets are short by more than $700 million a year, and ties civilian overruns partly to staffing shortages.
