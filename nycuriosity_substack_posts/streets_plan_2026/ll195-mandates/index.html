LL195 Benchmarks: Plan 1 vs. Plan 2

Minimum benchmarks set by Local Law 195 for each five-year plan period. Plan 1 was due December 1, 2021; Plan 2 is due December 1, 2026. These are legislative floors, not targets, DOT may exceed them but cannot lawfully fall short.

Legislative mandates
Benchmark Plan 1 (FY22–26, due Dec 2021) Plan 2 (FY27–31, due Dec 2026)
Protected bus lanes 150 miles total (20 mi yr 1; 30 mi/yr after) Protected lanes on all bus routes where feasible
Protected bike lanes 250 miles total (30 mi yr 1; 50 mi/yr after) Complete connected network; coverage index 100%
Transit signal priority (TSP) 750 intersections yr 1; 1,000/yr thereafter TSP at all feasible intersections
Bus stop upgrades 2,500 stops (500/yr) All stops where feasible
Intersection redesigns 2,000 intersections 2,000 additional intersections
Accessible pedestrian signals (APS) 2,500 signals (500/yr min) 2,500 additional signals
Pedestrian space 1,000,000 sq ft within 2 years —
Pedestrian ramps — 3,000 ramps at intersection corners
Commercial loading / truck routes Assess and amend citywide to support freight movement and curb access
Parking policies Develop policies prioritizing safety, transit, emissions reduction, and disability access

Source: Local Law 195 of 2019 (Intro. 1557-A); NYC Council Finance Division fiscal impact statement, Oct 2019.

About this data

  • These are legislative minimums, not targets. DOT is legally required to meet or exceed these benchmarks. The "where feasible" qualifier in Plan 2 gives DOT discretion where physical or operational constraints exist, but it does not reduce the general mandate.
  • Plan 1 was due December 1, 2021. NYC DOT published the Plan 1 streets plan on that date. Compliance with the annual delivery benchmarks (e.g., 30 mi of bus lanes/yr) is tracked separately, see the Compliance Tracker table.
  • Plan 2 is due December 1, 2026. The DOT must publish a new 5-year plan by that date, and the plan must meet the benchmarks in this table.
  • Source code and raw CSV files are available on GitHub.
