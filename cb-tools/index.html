Tools for New York’s 59 community boards, from who serves to what they decide

Community boards weigh in on land use, liquor licenses, street changes, and the city budget, and their members are unpaid appointees serving two-year terms. These tools track who serves on all 59 boards, teach the procedure that governs every meeting, and make four decades of board resolutions searchable. Members can get email alerts when a board they watch changes hands.
