Key insights

48%
Liquor licenses dominate the agenda
3,449 of 7,134 resolutions — nearly 1 in 2 — relate to liquor license applications reviewed by the State Liquor Authority (SLA).
2,285
Resolutions that mention the SLA
The State Liquor Authority is the single most-cited agency in full resolution text, ahead of DOT (440) and NYPD (368), reflecting CB3's active role in reviewing liquor license applications.
701
Resolutions in 2004 — the peak year
Board output has fallen sharply since the mid-2000s. Recent years see ~150–190 resolutions annually, less than a third of the 2004 peak.
99.6%
Pass rate among resolutions with recorded votes
Of 987 resolutions with recorded vote data, 983 passed and only 4 failed — with an average of 34.5 yes votes and 1.2 no votes per resolution.
520
Resolutions touch transportation or streets
520 resolutions mention DOT, bike lanes, traffic signals, bus lanes, crosswalks, or other street-related topics — making it the second-largest named category after liquor licenses.
3.3×
War and Peace novels worth of text
