Four in five state bills never leave committee

Of the 154,441 bills introduced in the 2009 to 2024 sessions, 123,410 ended in an Assembly or Senate committee. Pick a session, filter by chamber, stage, committee, sponsor or outcome, or search a print number or title. Each print number links to the bill on nysenate.gov.

Bills and resolutions come from the NY Senate’s Open Legislation data, which covers every session since 2009–10. Resolutions honor, commemorate or state a position and never become law; the Type filter separates them.

Status is the latest stage Open Legislation records for the bill. Each chamber prints its own copy of most bills, so when one chamber’s copy passes and is substituted, its twin in the other chamber usually stops in committee or on the floor. The 79.9% committee share counts both copies.

Sponsor is the prime sponsor on the bill. Committee is the committee the bill sits in or last sat in; bills the source lists with no committee show as “No committee”. Chapter is the chapter number the law received when the governor signed it.
