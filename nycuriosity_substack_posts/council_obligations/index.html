What the Council Asks of NYC Agencies

Every local law hands city agencies work: reports to write, rules to make, programs to run, inspections to carry out. These charts measure that workload using the NYCuriosity Legislation Implementation Tracker, which extracts every obligation from the text of the 2,138 local laws the Council has enacted since 2014.
