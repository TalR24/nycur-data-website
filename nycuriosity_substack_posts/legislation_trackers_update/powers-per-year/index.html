Duties and powers by year enacted

How many duties and powers the Council wrote into local laws each year since 2014, drawn from the Obligations and Powers trackers.

From 2014 to 2021 the Council granted 62 powers a year on average. From 2022 to 2025 it granted 33, while new duties rose from 560 a year to 605.

Method: Each provision of every local law enacted since 2014 is extracted with Claude (Anthropic's Haiku model) and sorted as a duty or a power by the model's label and by a fixed rule in code. Counts as of September 24 2026. Full methodology.
