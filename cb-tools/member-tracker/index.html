All 59 boards

New York City has 59 community boards, one per community district: 12 in Manhattan, 12 in the Bronx, 18 in Brooklyn, 14 in Queens, and 3 on Staten Island. Each board has up to 50 appointed members, a chair elected by the members, and a district manager on staff. Open a board for its full roster, officers, committee chairs, and term-limit dates.
