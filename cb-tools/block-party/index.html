NYC Community Board Resolutions Dashboard

Searchable, visualized, and downloadable resolutions from 26 community boards: 11 of Manhattan’s 12 and 15 of Brooklyn’s 18. This dashboard now lives on Block Party. Sources: NYC.gov MCB3 minutes, cbmanhattan.cityofnewyork.us (MCB2/4/10), cb7m.com Google Drive (MCB7), Airtable shared views (MCB1/5/6/11/12), Internet Archive CDX (MCB8); Brooklyn board minutes from nyc.gov, cbbrooklyn.cityofnewyork.us, board websites and public Google Drive folders (15 boards).
