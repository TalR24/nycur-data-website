The State Capacity Ecosystem

Good policy fails because the practitioners, technologists, researchers, and funders working to fix government are operating in silos, without a clear view of each other and with few collaboration pathways. We built a clear Directory of who is doing what. We added a collaborator map so you can Connect with the practitioners, technologists, researchers, and funders who can actually move your work forward. And we are hosting Events to make sure connections happen in real life.
