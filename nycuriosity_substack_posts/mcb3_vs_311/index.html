Does MCB3 Address What Residents Actually Report?

MCB3 passes formal resolutions on everything from liquor license applications to land-use reviews. Residents call 311 about noise, housing conditions, parking, and rodents. Are these two datasets pointing at the same problems — or talking past each other?

This analysis compares the topic composition of 7,134 MCB3 full-board resolutions (2004–2025) against 377,536 311 service requests from Community District 3 (2020–2026), mapped to the same 10 topic categories.

48% of MCB3 resolutions involve SLA / liquor licensing
0% of CB3 311 complaints involve SLA / liquor licensing
45% of 311 complaints are noise or public safety
4% of MCB3 resolutions address public safety
