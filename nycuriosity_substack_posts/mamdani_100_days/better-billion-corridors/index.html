A Better Billion: Top Priority Corridors

The NYU Marron Institute's A Better Billion report proposes 12 subway expansions — 41 new miles, 64 new stations — for roughly the same annual cost as fare-free buses citywide. At the April 7 panel, program director Eric Goldwyn highlighted four corridors as his top priorities, each anchored by an overcrowded bus corridor and the potential to unlock dense housing without any zoning changes.

Utica Avenue ridership (44,000/day): B46 Select Bus Service daily ridership figure, Brooklyn's busiest bus corridor. Source: MTA, cited in NYC Mayor's Office Select Bus Service announcement.

Cross-Bronx ridership (53,000/day): Combined Bx12 local and SBS weekday ridership. Source: MTA 2024 ridership data via Wikipedia/MTA records.

Utica Avenue housing estimate (17,000+ units): From Marron Institute report coverage; this is the only per-corridor housing figure confirmed in public reporting. Housing estimates for LIE, 125th Street, and Cross-Bronx corridors are in the full report but were not confirmed in public summaries reviewed for this post. Verify all per-corridor figures against the full report before publishing.

Phase decades reflect the order in which Goldwyn's report proposes building the corridors: Decade 1 is earliest priority, Decade 4 is furthest out in the 40-year plan.
