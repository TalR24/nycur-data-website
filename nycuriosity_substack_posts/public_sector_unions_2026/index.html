Mayor Mamdani's first contract round meets a budget that funds 1.25% raises

Personnel is half of what New York City spends. DC 37's contract expires November 6, 2026, the financial plan reserves 1.25% a year for raises, and the last round paid 3% a year. These six charts show where labor costs stand going in.

$550M–$600M City-funded cost of each 1% raise for the full workforce
1.25% annual raises funded in the financial plan
50.9% personnel share of the FY2027 adopted budget, down from 54.1% in FY2017
65.1% of public sector workers in NYC belong to a union
