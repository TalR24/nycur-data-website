Powers Tracker

New York laws signed since 2009 granted or extended 11,654 powers that state and local governments may use.

Each row is one power a signed law gave a government body, from authority to issue permits or set fees to the extension of a program an earlier law was set to end, with the public body that holds it and the sentence of the law it comes from.

How these records were made

Claude read the marked text of each signed law and listed the duties and powers it creates or widens, and the pipeline then drops reprinted text, conditions, definitions and duties of private parties before resolving each actor to a canonical public body. Two auditors check a blind sample of new records every night against a hand-verified set of 589 laws, where 93.1% of stored records are real duties or powers and 90.0% of the provisions in those laws are found.
