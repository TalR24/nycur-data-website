Boston built its digital groundwork between 2015 and 2017, years before its AI tools

The City put permit applications online in 2015, rebuilt its website in 2016 and opened a data hub in 2017. The two AI tools on this timeline, a permit search and a server that lets AI assistants query city data, date from fall 2025 and 2026.

What the City had in place before the AI tools

By the end of 2017 Boston had online permit applications, a rebuilt website and an open data hub. Its first rules for staff on generative AI came in 2023, and the two AI tools on this timeline, the permit search and Open Context, date from fall 2025 and early 2026.
