About this dataset: This tracker covers NYC City Council legislation where a fiscal impact statement was found on NYC Legistar and where the Finance Division estimated a non-zero fiscal impact. Bills with $0 estimated impact, unquantifiable impacts, or no fiscal impact statement are excluded. Figures are estimates prepared by the City Council Finance Division. Data is refreshed monthly. Net Impact = revenues minus expenditures minus capital costs; negative values represent a net cost to the city.
