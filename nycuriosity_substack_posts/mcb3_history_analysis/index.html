What has MCB3 focused on over time?

An analysis of 7,134 MCB3 full board resolutions from January 2002 through February 2026, examining how the board's topical focus has shifted, how SLA processing changes reshaped the resolution landscape, and how MCB3's formal agenda compares to the 311 complaints residents actually file.
