Context

MCB3's output peaked in 2004 at 701 resolutions and has declined steadily since — recent years average 150–190. MCB2's record begins in 2013 and runs in a different rhythm, climbing through the late 2010s and surging again from 2023 onward.

The COVID-19 pandemic in 2020 caused a notable dip on both boards as in-person meetings were suspended.
