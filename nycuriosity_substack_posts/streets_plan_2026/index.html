Methodology

About this data

  • Fiscal figures are "additional above baseline." The fiscal impact statement estimates the marginal cost of LL195 mandates — what NYC must spend beyond existing DOT commitments. They do not represent total DOT program budgets. For example, DOT had already committed to $59.2M/yr for 50 miles of bike lanes; the $11.8M figure in this dataset is only the additional cost above that.
  • Fiscal impact statement date: October 25, 2019 — prepared before the law took effect. Actual costs may differ from estimates as scope, inflation, and DOT priorities have changed since.
  • Compliance data is drawn from DOT Streets Plan annual progress reports (2024–2025) and from reporting in NYCuriosity's Streets Plan article. Bus stop upgrade figure (~20) is approximate based on DOT reporting.
  • Program costs do not sum exactly to fiscal summary totals. The fiscal impact statement lists programs in narrative form; not all items are broken into plan-1/plan-2 sub-totals. The summary table is taken directly from the official fiscal impact statement; program breakdown figures are extracted from individual program narrative paragraphs.
  • Pedestrian space surplus (−$73.3M) is a cost offset, not a revenue item. DOT's existing commitment exceeds the LL195 mandate, freeing up funds for other programs.
  • Source code and raw CSV files are available on GitHub.
