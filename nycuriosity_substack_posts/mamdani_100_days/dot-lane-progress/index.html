DOT Lane Progress vs. Legal Mandate

NYC Local Law 195 (the Streets Plan) requires DOT to install 50 miles of protected bike lanes and 30 miles of protected bus lanes every year. In 2024, DOT built 29 bike lane miles (58% of the mandate) and 13.5 bus lane miles (45% of the mandate). Incoming Chief Strategy Officer Tiffany-Ann Taylor inherits both gaps — plus the stakeholder tensions that slow delivery.

2024 actuals: 29 miles of protected bike lanes and 13.5 miles of protected bus lanes installed in 2024. Source: NYC DOT 2024 Record of Accomplishment. The 13.5 miles of protected bus lanes was described by DOT as more progress than any prior single year.

Legal mandates (50 mi / 30 mi per year): Set by NYC Local Law 195 of 2019 (the NYC Streets Plan). Bike lane mandate: 50 miles of protected bike lanes annually. Bus lane mandate: 30 miles of protected bus lanes annually. Source: City & State NY.

Cumulative note: Over the prior three years, DOT installed a total of 87.5 miles of protected bike lanes — an average of ~29 miles/year, well below the 50-mile annual mandate. The gap compounds each year the mandate is missed.
