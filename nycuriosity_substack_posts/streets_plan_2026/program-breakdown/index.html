About this data

  • All figures are "additional above baseline." The fiscal impact statement estimates the marginal cost of LL195 mandates, not the total DOT program budget. For example, DOT had already committed to $59.2M/yr for 50 miles of bike lanes; the $11.8M figure for protected bike lanes is only the cost above that existing commitment.
  • Pedestrian space surplus (−$73.3M) is a cost offset, not a revenue item. DOT's existing commitment to 1.5M sq ft of pedestrian space already exceeds the LL195 mandate, freeing up funds for other programs.
  • Program costs do not sum exactly to the fiscal summary totals. The fiscal impact statement lists programs in narrative form; not all items are broken into plan-1/plan-2 sub-totals. The fiscal summary table is taken directly from the official statement; program figures are extracted from individual program narrative paragraphs.
