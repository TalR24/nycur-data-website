Budget September 2026

Two in three public workers in NYC are in a union, against one in seven private workers

Public sector union density in New York City is 65.1%, nearly double the national rate of 32.7%. Private sector density in the city is 14.3%. One in five workers in the five boroughs belongs to a union.
