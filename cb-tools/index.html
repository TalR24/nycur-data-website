NYC Community Board Tools

Working tools for improving how NYC community boards run: a member and leadership tracker for all 59 boards, a parliamentary procedure helper, an AI meeting review, and a citywide board scorecard, built alongside NYCuriosity’s recommendations for the Civic Engagement Commission’s Better Public Meetings study, plus Block Party, the searchable archive of four decades of Manhattan community board resolutions and meetings.
