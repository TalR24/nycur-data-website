The PIT Crew is the tip of the iceberg

We built working prototypes for three of the Mayor's top priorities. Enabling NYC's 300,000-person workforce with AI is how the Mayor's affordability agenda actually ships.

Mayor Mamdani's affordability agenda runs through a government that moves on paperwork: applications, licenses, benefit rules, inspection records. That is exactly the work AI made cheap almost overnight. The prototypes below were each built in hours, for a few dollars in token spend, against the mayor's stated priorities. The full argument is in the post; the working tools live here.

Read the post ↗
Three of the mayor's priorities, three working prototypes

Each tool answers a concrete operational question: where the five city-owned grocery stores should go to close the largest food-access gaps, how a small-business owner responds to a summons, and which approvals stand between an affordable-housing idea and lease-up (40 processes across 18 offices and agencies, per the mayor's COGE charter revision committee).

Making groceries cheaper
Grocery store siting tool
Identifies disadvantaged census tracts with high food insecurity and strong transit connectivity, then scores vacant city-owned lots as candidate store sites. Adjust the scoring weights and the top sites re-rank live. Built on public data; illustrative, not an acquisition list.
Open the prototype →
Ownership & small business
NYC Summons Navigator
A plain-language guide for business owners who received a City summons: pick the issuing agency, enter the dates on the ticket, and see your deadlines and all four response options (cure, fight, pay, default) explained, including how to fix a missed hearing.
Open the prototype →
Building affordable housing
NYC Affordable Housing Approvals Navigator
Pick one of three real Brooklyn affordable-housing projects (drawn from HPD and DOB NOW open data), set the financing and site facts, and get a phased roadmap of every approval between you and lease-up, including the ones the subsidy itself triggers.
Open the prototype →
