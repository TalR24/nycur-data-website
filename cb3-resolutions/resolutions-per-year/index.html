MCB3 Resolutions Per Year, 2004–2025

Annual resolution output from Manhattan Community Board 3. Excludes 2002–2003 (partial records) and 2026 (year in progress). Peak output was 701 resolutions in 2004; recent years average 150–190.

MCB3 resolutions per year, 2004–2025. 2026 excluded (year in progress). Source: NYC.gov MCB3 meeting minutes. Created by Tal Roded · NYCuriosity

Context

The sharp drop from the mid-2000s peak likely reflects multiple factors: changes in how the board packages resolutions (e.g., omnibus vs. individual motions), shifts in how liquor license applications are processed, and evolving board practices over time. The COVID-19 pandemic in 2020 caused a notable dip to 150 resolutions — the lowest on record — as in-person meetings were suspended.

2004's peak of 701 resolutions is more than 4× the 2022 low of 158. Even accounting for procedural differences, this suggests the board's formal output has contracted substantially over two decades.

Data

7,134 resolutions across 268 MCB3 full board meetings, 2004–2025. Source: NYC.gov MCB3 meeting minutes. 2002–2003 and 2026 excluded from this chart.
