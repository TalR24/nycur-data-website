Members

One email a month when a board you watch changes hands

NYCuriosity members pick boards or whole boroughs. When the monthly refresh finds a new chair, a new district manager, a committee chair handoff, or members joining or leaving a published roster, members get a single email listing every match.

Already a member?

Sign in with the email on your membership. You will get a one-time code by email, then land on the alert preferences page to pick your boards, boroughs, and change types.

Sign-in is a one-time email code through Cloudflare Access; there is no password. If your code never arrives or your membership email changed, reply to any NYCuriosity email or write to troded24@gmail.com.
