Ninety-Four Years at the Subway's Crossroads

West 4th Street–Washington Square opened in 1932 with an entire lower level built for a line that would not exist for another eight years. This timeline traces the station from that bet on the future through 27 years without express tracks, a failed first generation of elevators, and the renewal completed in May 2026.

Building the crossroads1932–1940
1932 Sept 10
The upper level opens with the city-built IND Eighth Avenue Line
Four island platforms and eight tracks on two levels, with the entire lower level built for a line that existed only on paper.
IND planners added "West" to avoid confusion with South 4th Street, a planned Brooklyn hub that was never built. The station is named for a ghost.
NYU asked for purple tiles and was refused. Tile color was a navigation system, and West 4th's green bands marked an express stop.
1936 Jan 1
E trains borrow the empty lower level
The waiting shell carries its first trains four years before its own line opens.
New here:E
1940 Dec 15
The Sixth Avenue Line arrives, eight years after its platforms
West 4th becomes the only station where the IND's two Manhattan trunk lines meet. It still is.
Two decades pass with no express tracks
Completing the design1961–1967
1961 Apr 19
Ground breaks on the missing express tracks
A $22M project threads two new tracks beneath the existing locals and around the PATH tubes.
1967 Nov 26
B and D expresses finally run
After 27 local-only years, the station's full eight-track design is used as drawn.

1970
Escalators finally connect the two levels
Two up-only escalators arrive, five years after the Manhattan Borough President first requested them.
1978–1990
The JFK Express stops at the lower level
A premium-fare train links the station directly to the airport for twelve years.

2004 July
The station joins the National Register of Historic Places
Recognized as a signature work of the IND, the subway system New York City built itself.
2005 April
Three new elevators make the station accessible, then fail almost immediately
Out of service a cumulative nine months in year one; one stays closed 134 straight days.
2010
The M joins and completes today's roster
Service changes route the M through the Chrystie Street Connection to Sixth Avenue.
