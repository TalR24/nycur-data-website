How to Be an Effective NYC Community Board Member

The whole playbook on a single page. Master process, build relationships, and act where decisions are made, in committee, early.

The three levers of influence

  • Coalitions. Know each member's leanings; ally with the aligned, bridge to the rest, trade support over time.
  • Stakeholders. Build relationships with council staff (discretionary $), agency liaisons, the BP's office, and your District Manager.
  • Participation. Show up, speak prepared, build a record. Credibility compounds meeting by meeting.

Where the decision is really made

HIGH

Full board
Usually ratified, little debate.
  • Influence flows backward, engage early.
  • Track the committee calendar, not just the full board.
  • No appointment yet? Join a committee as a public member.

Anatomy of a resolution

Title
One sentence: what it's about.
Whereas… (×N)
Context, evidence, reasoning, agreements.
Be it resolved
The specific, concrete ask.
  • Whoever drafts, wins, volunteer to write it.
  • Name the agency, location, and action.

Robert's Rules, the bare minimum

  • Quorum = majority of appointed members present.
  • To pass: yes > no + abstentions (abstaining isn't neutral).
  • Be recognized by the Chair; votes are public record.
  • Four motions do the work: main, amend, table/call the question, point of order.
  • No proxies, absentee, or phone votes, be in the room.
The one rule that matters most: if you care about a policy coming before your board, influence it at the committee stage, before positions harden. And don't skip the District Needs Statement (committees draft in June): it's the rare CB tool that agencies are obligated to answer in the city budget.

Quick-start checklist

  • Pick one committee; attend every meeting.
  • Learn the committee calendar; engage early.
  • Volunteer to draft a resolution.
  • Make a clean main motion.
  • Meet the District Manager & a council staffer.
  • Map your board's allies & skeptics.
  • Put a priority in the District Needs Statement (June).
  • Stay clean, disclose, recuse, follow COIB.
