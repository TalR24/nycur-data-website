CSO Savings vs. IBO Budget Options

How do the Mayor's agency Chief Savings Officer plans stack up against the IBO-identified budget options analyzed in NYCuriosity's January 2026 post? All figures represent estimated annual impact in FY2027 dollars (CSO) or as estimated by IBO based on FY2024–2026 data. NYC FY2026 adopted budget: $119.7 billion.

CSO figures: All CSO savings figures ($1.7B total, $196.5M FY27 highlighted, $49.6M FY26 highlighted) are from the Office of the Mayor's announcement of the Chief Savings Officer program, March 20, 2026. Individual agency reports have not yet been released publicly.

IBO figures: Represent estimated annual revenue or savings impact. Camera fines estimate assumes no behavioral change; actual collections may be lower if fines deter repeat violations. Façade penalty and MSG figures are NYC Department of Finance/IBO estimates.

Sources: CSO figures from the Office of the Mayor announcement, March 20, 2026. IBO budget options from NYCuriosity, January 5, 2026, based on NYC Independent Budget Office analysis. FY26 city budget ($119.7B) from NYC OMB. Created by Tal Roded · NYCuriosity
