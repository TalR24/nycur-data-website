Suggestions to Improve NYC Community Boards

Data behind NYCuriosity's recommendations for the Civic Engagement Commission's Better Public Meetings study: what all 59 community boards actually get to run on, how their funding fell behind City Hall's over the past decade, and the full 14-point reform table from the article.

$22.3M Combined FY2027 budgets of all 59 boards, 0.019% of the city budget
$2.63 Per resident per year for the bodies charged with representing them
30% vs 61% Board budget growth vs. the Mayor's Office since FY2017
