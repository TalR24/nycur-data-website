Top Topics by Resolution Count

Which subjects do the selected community boards address most? Categorized by keyword matching against full resolution text. Stacked segments show each board's share of the combined total.

Top topics in NYC Community Board resolutions. Source: Manhattan Community Board meeting PDFs. Created by Tal Roded · NYCuriosity

Methodology

Each resolution's full text (from full_resolutions.csv) was matched against keyword lists for each category. Each resolution is counted in at most one category (first-match-wins). Categories with zero matches in the current selection are hidden.

Liquor licenses dominate on most boards because every SLA application generates its own resolution. This is a structural feature of how community boards review nightlife, not a comment on any board's priorities.

Data

Resolutions across seven of Manhattan's twelve Community Boards. Sources: NYC.gov MCB3 minutes, cbmanhattan.cityofnewyork.us (MCB2/4/10), cb7m.com Drive (MCB7), Internet Archive CDX (MCB5/8).
