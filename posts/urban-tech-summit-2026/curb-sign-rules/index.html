New York City's curb rules live in 3,788 different sign texts

NYC DOT publishes every parking sign as open data, with each rule held as one line of text. No field holds the days, hours, vehicle type or time limit, so software has to read the sign the way a driver does.

Download CSV (members)

What a sign record holds

The rule on each sign is a single text field. Three examples from the dataset:

  • 2 HMP SATURDAY 8AM-7PM <->
  • TRUCK (SYMBOL) TRUCK LOADING ONLY MONDAY-FRIDAY 8AM-6PM --> (SUPERSEDES SP-449CA & SP-70BA)
  • NO PARKING (SANITATION BROOM SYMBOL) TUESDAY FRIDAY 11:30AM-1PM <-> (SUPERSEDES SP-361C)

Fifteen cities and counties are on the curb data standard's user list, and New York City is not

The Open Mobility Foundation lists these places as using, developing for or planning to use its Curb Data Specification, a free standard for publishing curb rules as data.

Sources: NYC DOT's Parking Regulation Locations and Signs dataset on NYC Open Data, queried November 17, 2026, which the agency describes as covering parking regulation signs among the approximately 1,000,000 signs it manages.

Method: the 3,788 different values of the sign description field were grouped by keyword, checked in this order: street cleaning, bus, truck and commercial loading, metered and time-limited parking, schools, permits and agencies, taxi and carshare, no standing, no stopping, no parking. A text that matches none is counted as other. Each text is counted once.
