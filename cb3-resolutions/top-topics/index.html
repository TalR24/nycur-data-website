Top Topics by Resolution Count

Which subjects does MCB3 address most? Categorized by keyword matching against the full text of 7,134 resolutions from 268 meetings (2004–2025). One resolution may span multiple categories.

Top topics in MCB3 resolutions, 2004–2025. Categorized by keyword matching against full resolution text; some resolutions appear in multiple categories. Source: NYC.gov MCB3 meeting minutes. Author's analysis. Created by Tal Roded · NYCuriosity

Methodology

Each resolution's full text (from full_resolutions.csv) was matched against keyword lists for each category. A resolution is counted once per category regardless of how many matching keywords appear. Because some resolutions touch multiple categories, category totals do not sum to 7,134.

Using full resolution text rather than titles significantly improves SLA detection: early-year SLA denials were often recorded with short vote-only titles ("To deny renewal... due to nonappearance") that contained no SLA keywords, but their full text includes terms like "nonappearance," "method of operation," and "State Liquor Authority."

Data

7,134 resolutions across 268 MCB3 full board meetings, 2004–2025. Source: NYC.gov MCB3 meeting minutes.
