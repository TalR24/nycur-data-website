Fiscal Impacts Tracker

What Council legislation costs the city

Council bills and resolutions whose fiscal impact statement put a price on them: every local law enacted since 2014, plus pending bills from 2026 (Legistar's search does not reach earlier pending bills). Each bill counts once, at its annual cost in a year of full implementation; pilots count over the years their statement prices.
