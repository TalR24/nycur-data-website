About Us
NYCuriosity Data / State Capacity Ecosystem

The State Capacity Ecosystem

Practitioners, technologists, researchers, and funders are rebuilding government's ability to deliver. This is the shared infrastructure for that work: a map of the field, a community of builders, reusable methods, and the writing and events that carry them.

Our mission

Connect the people working to make government deliver, and prove what they can build together.

Our vision

A government with the talent, tools, and operating capacity to deliver for the people it serves.

What we do
Events
The community meets in a room and leaves with working tools.

Hackathons are the proof engine: our first build night put 80+ builders in 20 teams and produced 20 working tools in under two hours. Demo nights and salon-style discussions are next as the calendar grows. Sponsors get a checklist that spells out exactly who owns what.

Ecosystem Databases
The field is big. Now you can see all of it.

A database of 300+ organizations working on state capacity, built by the SCE team and visualized three ways: a searchable Organization Directory of who does what, an Affinity Map that scores which orgs share problems and funders, and an Opportunities & Connections board of the people behind the work. Start with a question and the search will point you to the orgs and people closest to it.

Community & Platform
Everything we learn becomes something you can join or reuse.

The people layer and the reusable layer in one place. The community Slack is open to join, the Substack ships working prototypes alongside the writing, the playbooks let anyone run our event formats without us in the room, and the proof points show what one build night can produce.
